BRAND STYLE GUIDE

Vanessa Stoykov

A reference for the social team. Everything below is taken directly from the website so posts, stories and reels sit alongside it without looking like a different brand.

01

Colour

Navy
#001E60
Primary. Headlines, body text, buttons, footer.
Blush Pink
#F09491
Accent. Eyebrow labels, underlines, newsletter panel.
Pale Pink
#FBE0D4
Gradient partner to blush. Never used alone.
Warm Stone
#F7F5F2
Section backgrounds, quote blocks, footer.
Off White
#FDFCFB
Page ground. Not pure white.
Slate
#33456f
Body copy. Softer than navy for long text.
DO
  • Navy on off-white as the default pairing.
  • Pink for one accent per post, not several.
  • Warm stone when you need a second background.
DON'T
  • Pink body copy — it fails contrast at small sizes.
  • Pure white or pure black backgrounds.
  • More than two background colours in one post.
02

Type

HEADLINES
Money Decisions Are Life Decisions
Bebas Neue. All caps by nature. Tight line height (0.95–1.15). Never italic, never letter-spaced out.
EYEBROW LABEL
FREE RESOURCES FOR LIFE'S BIGGEST MOMENTS
Inter Bold, 11–13px, uppercase, 0.08em letter-spacing, always pink. Sits above a headline.
BODY

Money is rarely just about money. It's about relationships. Family. Freedom. Security. Ageing. Starting again.

Inter Regular, 15–17px, line height 1.7–1.75, slate. Left aligned.
BUTTON
TAKE THE FREE QUIZ NOW
Inter Bold, 12–13px, uppercase, 0.06em tracking. Fully rounded pill, navy fill, white text.
03

Shape and space

Rounded, always
Cards and image boxes use 20px radius. Buttons and pills use 999px. Nothing on the site has a sharp corner.
Soft shadow only
A single low shadow in navy at 8–10% opacity. No dark drop shadows, no glows.
Generous margins
Let copy breathe. Empty space is intentional — don't fill it with extra graphics or stats.
04

Photography

  • Real photography of Vanessa — speaking, at her desk, in conversation.
  • Warm, natural light. Full colour, no filters or duotones.
  • Rounded corners on every image placement.
  • Crop so the subject reads clearly at thumbnail size.
  • Never crop text over her face or head.
AVOID
  • Generic finance stock imagery — charts, coins, piggy banks.
  • Heavy gradient overlays.
  • Emoji as graphic elements.
05

Voice

Money is rarely just about money.

The line the brand keeps returning to. Every post should sound like it could sit under it.

HOW WE SOUND
  • Direct and warm. Speaks to one person, not an audience.
  • Plain words over financial jargon.
  • Names the real moment — divorce, inheritance, ageing parents.
  • Never shames anyone for their money decisions.
  • Australian spelling.
HOW WE DON'T
  • Hype, urgency or scarcity language.
  • Guru tone or promises of specific returns.
  • Clickbait questions with no substance behind them.
  • Specific financial advice — always point to a professional.
