Money Stress Relief: How Pets Help You Reset and Find Calm

By Vanessa Stoykov

If you’ve ever found yourself overwhelmed by money worries, deadlines, or the endless ‘to do’ list, you’re not alone. Financial stress is a daily reality for many Australians, especially after 50. The pressure to support family, juggle bills, and stay on top of everything can leave you feeling stretched to the limit. But there’s an unlikely source of relief hiding in plain sight: animals.

Why Animals Offer Real Money Stress Relief

When you’re deep in financial anxiety, it can feel impossible to take a break from the constant mental chatter. Pets; and even brief encounters with animals, interrupt that cycle. They draw you back to the present, even if only for a moment. Research shows that spending time with animals lowers cortisol (the stress hormone) and can reduce anxiety. According to a 2022 RSPCA Australia report, 61% of households say pets help them manage daily stress.

Unlike work, bills, or family obligations, animals don’t care about your productivity or your worries. They simply offer gentle company and a reminder to breathe. That’s something no spreadsheet can do.

Small Moments, Big Impact

You don’t need to own a pet to feel this benefit. Maybe a neighbour’s dog wanders past during a walk, or you spot a cat stretched out in a sunny window. Even a few minutes with a friendly animal can soften a tough day. Those small pauses aren’t a cure for your problems, but they give your mind a break from the constant grind.

Pets and Your Financial Reality

Of course, pets come with real costs. Veterinary bills, food, and care all add up. Many people worry about taking on more responsibility when they’re already financially stretched. It’s a valid concern, no animal should be brought home unless you’re confident you can provide for them. Vet bills can hit when you least expect it, and caring for a pet is a genuine long-term commitment.

But the good news? You can benefit from time with animals without the financial burden of ownership. Community programs, animal cafes, and even volunteering at shelters are all ways to experience that stress-relieving presence. Look for local opportunities, many cities now offer pet therapy days, animal-assisted activities, or simply spaces where animals are welcome.

Action Steps: How to Build Animal Calm Into Your Life

  • Pause when you see a pet. Even a short hello to a dog on the street can shift your mood.
  • Visit a local cat café or pet-friendly park. These spaces are designed for animal interaction, no ownership required.
  • Volunteer at an animal shelter. Many organisations welcome help with walking dogs or socialising cats.
  • Talk to loved ones about sharing time with their pets. It’s a simple way to connect and reset, together.

Finding Balance When Life Feels Too Much

Stepping away from constant money stress isn’t about ignoring your responsibilities. It’s about protecting your own wellbeing so you can face challenges with a clear head. Those moments of animal calm are more than just pleasant distractions, they’re a form of self-care that helps you reset.

If you’re struggling to manage financial pressure, it’s okay to ask for help. Free, confidential support is available through the National Debt Helpline and Financial Counselling Australia. And if you need tailored advice, I can help you find a trusted financial planner through my network.

You deserve relief from relentless money stress. Sometimes, all it takes is a moment with a cat, a dog, or even a friendly animal you meet by chance, to remind you that not everything needs to be solved right now. Give yourself permission to pause, reset, and take the next step forward.
