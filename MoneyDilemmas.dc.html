ABOUT THE SHOW

One dilemma. One honest conversation.

Most people will never have the opportunity to sit down with some of the country's leading minds and ask them to help unpack a major money decision. Money Dilemmas changes that.

Hosted by Vanessa Stoykov, the series brings exceptional experts together with real-life financial dilemmas, exploring the choices, consequences and opportunities that shape people's lives. From family and relationships to business, property, wealth and retirement, no subject is off limits.
